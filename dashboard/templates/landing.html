How it works

From CV to sent applications in four steps

  1. 1

    Upload your CV

    Your profile is drafted from it in both languages. You check every sentence and choose a writing style.

  2. 2

{# ──────────────────────────────── FAQ ──────────────────────────────── #}
FAQ

Questions, answered

Does Internix send emails without asking me?

Never. Internix only prepares drafts. You read them, edit them if you want, and choose which ones to send.

Will the emails all look the same?

No. Each one quotes what that particular company does and matches it to the most relevant part of your experience. You also choose among five writing styles.

Which AI do I need?

Any OpenAI-compatible provider. Groq offers a free tier that works well. You paste your key once in Settings; it's stored encrypted and used only for your account.

Can I use it if I'm not in computer science?

Yes. Your profile is drafted from your own CV, whatever your field — finance, marketing, engineering, law or research.

What if a company's website is in French?

The email is written in French automatically, with French wording and a French greeting. You can switch any email to English with one click.

Which email address are the applications sent from?

Your own — through Google sign-in, a Gmail app password, or your university or company SMTP server. Replies come straight to you.
