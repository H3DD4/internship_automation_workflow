{# -------------------------------------------------------------- faq #}
FAQ

Questions, answered

Does Ntern send emails on its own?

No. It prepares drafts. You read them, edit them if you want, and choose which ones to send.

Isn't automation bad for quality?

Usually, yes — the same text is sent to everyone. Ntern researches each company separately, matches it to your CV and checks every claim, so a long list still gets personal emails.

Can I use my own cover letter?

Yes. Keep your own wording and Ntern fills in only what is specific to each company. You can also pick one of the {{ styles | length }} styles, or switch a single email to another style at any time.

Will my emails end up in spam?

Ntern does what keeps you out of it: it sends from your own mailbox, one plain-text email at a time, with random gaps and a daily limit, and every email is different. It also flags addresses that bounce.

Which languages are supported?

English and French. Ntern picks the language from each company's website automatically, and you can switch any single email with the EN / FR button.

What do I need to start?

Your CV, a list of companies with an email address each, and an AI key — several providers offer a free tier that is enough to begin.

How do I create an account?

{% if google %}Click “Continue with Google”. Your account is created, you are signed in, and your Gmail is connected for sending — all in one step. You can also sign up with an email and a password.{% else %}Sign up with your email and a password, then connect your mailbox in Settings.{% endif %}

Can other people see my data?

No. Every account has its own workspace, keys and mailbox connection.
