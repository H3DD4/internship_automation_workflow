{# -------------------------------------------------------------- faq #}
FAQ

Questions, answered

Does Internix send emails on its own?

No. It prepares drafts. You read them, edit them if you want, and choose which ones to send.

What do I need to start?

Your CV, a list of companies with an email address each, and an AI key — Groq offers a free tier that is enough to begin.

How do I create an account?

{% if google %}Click “Continue with Google”. Your account is created, you are signed in, and your Gmail is connected for sending — all in one step. You can also sign up with an email and a password.{% else %}Sign up with your email and a password, then connect your Gmail in Settings.{% endif %}

Which languages are supported?

English and French. Internix picks the language from each company's website, and you can switch any single email with the EN / FR button.

What file format does my company list need?

A CSV or Excel file with a company name and an email column. A template is provided, and the import tells you exactly which rows need fixing.

Can other people see my data?

No. Every account has its own workspace, keys and mailbox connection.
