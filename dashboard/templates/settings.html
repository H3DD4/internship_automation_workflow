AI models

Internix uses AI to read each company's website and compare it with your CV. You connect your own free key, so your usage never competes with anyone else's. The emails themselves are built from your approved wording, not written by the AI.

Connect more than one, and tick several models. Free plans limit how many requests you can make per minute. Every provider and model you add joins your pool: when one is busy or out of free quota, Internix moves to the next by itself — so your preparation keeps going instead of waiting.
